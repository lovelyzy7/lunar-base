Multi-level selection: ALL → chapter → difficulty; ticking a parent checks every quest below it. Use the toolbar buttons to complete the chosen scope. Each clear replays lunar-tear's real finish — it grants the first-clear, mission and drop rewards, marks the quest cleared, unlocks the next quest/difficulty, and records its side-story scenario, exactly like playing it. Already-cleared quests are skipped. A backup is taken automatically before every change. Selection is remembered across page reloads.

Main-story quests should be cleared in order. Skipping a quest's predecessors can leave your story progress inconsistent (the next chapter may not open correctly). Events are self-contained — tick the lower difficulty before the higher one so the unlock cascades.
